## 1. Context & Motivation

During the Step 2R provenance and diagnostic audit (F8 / R3), two structural discrepancies in Suite v0 were identified:

1. **Synthetic Stand-Ins for Public Benchmarks**:
   - `science_mcq` and `knowledge_mcq` were labeled as `arc_challenge` and `mmlu` in suite metadata, but were generated by local procedural Python generators (`science_mcq_generator.py` and `knowledge_mcq_generator.py`). Verbatim match against official ARC-Challenge and MMLU questions is **0%**.
   - `math` and `code` were generated with 100% synthetic templates (6 math families, 36 code families). While the Step 1 documentation discussed GSM8K and HumanEval/MBPP, zero public items exist in the actual suite.
2. **Memorization & Verifier Overfitting Risk**:
   - With only 6 math procedural templates and 36 code families, Member B's learned verifier (Step 3 / Step 6) risks memorizing template-specific syntactic idioms rather than learning generalized reasoning.
3. **50 Exposed Locked Knowledge-QA Items**:
   - In Step 1, 50 locked knowledge-QA items were temporarily exposed in an intermediate commit log, necessitating replacement before Step 10 execution.

Because Candidate Baseline runs (R4) and the Frontier Model $F$ paced baseline have not yet started, **now is the most cost-effective and clean moment to rebuild the suite**.

---

## 2. Rebuild Specifications for Suite v2.0

If approved by the co-owners, Suite v2.0 will implement the following:

### A. Real Public Benchmark Ingestion (Math & Code)
- **Math (200 items/split)**: 50% verified real GSM8K problems (Cobbe et al., 2021) + 50% freshly generated synthetic word problems.
- **Code (100 items/split)**: 50% verified MBPP problems (Google Research, 2021) + 50% freshly generated algorithmic functions.
- **Science MCQ & Knowledge MCQ**:
  - Ingest genuine public ARC-Challenge (CC BY-SA 4.0) and MMLU (MIT) test items, or explicitly re-classify the current procedural generators as `synthetic_science` and `synthetic_mmlu` in metadata to eliminate misleading provenance labels.

### B. Template Family Metadata & Family-Level Hold-Out
- Every synthetic and real item will carry explicit `metadata.template_family` (or `rule_family`).
- **Verifier Hold-Out**: The train split ($N=1,200$) and validation/locked splits will partition templates at the **family level**, ensuring that Member B's verifier is tested on unseen template structures.

### C. Quarantine & Replacement of Exposed Locked Items
- The 50 exposed locked knowledge-QA items will be replaced with fresh Wikipedia abstract extractions generated from unexposed entity pages.
- Fresh partition generation with seed pinning.
